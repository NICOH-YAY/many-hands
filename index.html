A game about meaning

Many Hands

You are one stonecutter in a crowd raising a tower that needs more hands than will ever meet. Twelve years. Three efforts a year. Lay a stone for the Tower, or light a candle at your own Hearth. Nothing says which is right, and nothing is scored.

Keys: T or 1 lay a stone · H or 2 light a candle · Backspace undo · Enter sleep / continue. Or just tap the buttons.

The crowd is simulated and the page says so: no one is online. Four switches change the rules (counter, your mark, horizon, storms), and a table of your runs shows what each one did. Play a few ways, then ask what you felt.

Built for COMS E6998 (AI in Practice: Game Design and Development, Columbia, Fall 2026) as a minigame for the Meaning motivator, drawing on McGonigal 2011 and Schell 2019. Drawn entirely in code; no assets, no network calls. Source on GitHub.
